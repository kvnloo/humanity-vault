# Limitless Protocol × ATLAS: a compounding human-capability stack

Product-owner draft, 2026-10-20 America/Chicago / 2026-10-21 UTC. Continuation of [Human Capability Loop #1](https://github.com/kvnloo/humanity-vault/issues/1), not a new product backlog or runtime. The [z0 ownership contract](https://github.com/kvnloo/z0/issues/15) remains the architecture authority; [NOOP #90](https://github.com/kvnloo/noop/issues/90) remains the historical Limitless traceability index. This document proposes delivery order and integration acceptance. Nothing here certifies a deployed system, scientific benefit, exhaustive history retrieval, or an approved upstream change.

## 1. Product thesis and first principles

The useful interpretation of “NZT as software” is **compounding human capability with less friction and more agency**: help someone understand, remember, create, recover, collaborate, and act on goals they actually choose. It is not a pharmacological claim, limitless productivity mandate, or a new physiological score.

**Limitless** is the personal learning/evidence loop. **ATLAS** is the physical environment and sustenance loop: spaces, food, movement, cultivation, tools, energy and eventually appropriately controlled robotics. They share provenance and experiment contracts, not one giant database or one autonomous authority. **ACE** supplies a possible human/expert/facility proving ground; **Civilization** can make learning/progress legible and playful. These remain separate owning projects.

A person is not an objective function to be silently optimized. The user chooses a changing portfolio of valued outcomes and non-negotiable constraints. Candidate dimensions include durable competence, physical vitality, creative contribution, relationships/belonging, autonomy, time/financial resilience, and enjoyment. Relationship and meaning measures are optional self-authored reflections, not inferred personality labels, private-message surveillance or automated social ranking.

Use a vector of outcomes, uncertainty and costs, not a universal weighted “flourishing index.” Sleep, consent, accessibility, privacy, control and attention limits are constraints, not penalties the optimizer may trade away for more output. Do not treat wearable proxies, software task completion, Anki self-ratings, independent transfer tests and felt wellbeing as interchangeable measurements.

Three mechanisms should earn their complexity independently:

1. **Reduce avoidable friction:** remember intent, prepare context, improve access, remove unnecessary steps and interruptions.
2. **Improve learning and decisions:** grounded explanations, retrieval practice, calibrated feedback, skilled human collaboration and appropriately timed optional assistance.
3. **Learn what genuinely helps:** prospective hypotheses, fair baselines, delayed outcomes, negative evidence and user-approved adaptation.

A simple routine that helps is preferable to a sophisticated system that repeatedly asks the user to maintain it. Less use of the app can be a success.

## 2. Two learning loops, different clocks

### Software autoresearch — faster, sandboxed

`research question → evidence and counterevidence → bounded candidate → isolated execution → frozen evaluation → review → opt-in canary → monitor → keep/revert`

Evolution Lab may search permitted prompt, retrieval, interface and policy parameters using synthetic/public or expressly authorized local evidence. Candidate search cannot edit its evaluator, leak the held-out item bank, change user goals, broaden permissions, buy providers, deploy globally or erase unfavorable trials. Record all attempts, seeds, exact sources, evaluator revisions, compute/attention costs and reasons for rejection. A timeout or missing observation is not a zero-cost success.

### Human/physical research — slower, explicitly consented

`chosen goal → current evidence and uncertainty → one approved low-risk comparison → exposure/adherence record → wait for specified outcome → independent assessment → user/expert review`

A seven-day outcome cannot be accelerated by generating seven days of synthetic human data. A simulator is useful for debugging a study, not proving a human effect. Time, carryover, practice and lasting learning matter. Documentary **A/B/A/B retrieval** is not an instruction to use ABAB crossover for every human study; learned knowledge is not necessarily reversible. Choose matched novel-item families, prospective comparisons or another justified design rather than assuming a washout makes a learned skill disappear. Method references: [COS preregistration](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg) and [CENT N-of-1 reporting](https://www.bmj.com/content/350/bmj.h1793); applicability must be checked per study.

### Shared lifecycle

`PROPOSED → SOURCE_GROUNDED → CONTRACT_TESTED → SIMULATED → SHADOW → APPROVED_PILOT → DELAYED_OUTCOME_AVAILABLE → INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED → LIMITED_ADOPTION`

Branches also include `INCONCLUSIVE`, `REFUTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `REVOKED` and `ROLLED_BACK`. Later evidence can downgrade an earlier conclusion. Passing code tests never moves a human-benefit claim directly to adoption. Existing native evidence/permission systems implement these distinctions; this is not a request for a new global state-machine service.

## 3. Product experience: one coherent journey, several hosts

The default experience should be modest: **one user-chosen goal, one optional useful next action, one honest recap**. The broader stack exists behind it.

- **Capture once:** select a goal, source, time/attention budget and boundaries. Voice, keyboard and touch produce editable drafts; no invisible diary writes.
- **Prepare quietly:** retrieve only authorized source fragments, preserve origin/version and show uncertainty. Prefer silence when context is absent or a prompt is not useful.
- **Act deliberately:** Do / Later / Skip / Edit / Why / Off. Two minutes is an optional initiation aid, not a rule limiting meaningful work or a promise of efficacy.
- **Learn without dependency:** scaffold understanding, then test later without the same hints or AI assistance; preserve user-authored work and genuine skill.
- **Reflect briefly:** “What happened? What is uncertain? What would distinguish the explanations? Keep, change, or stop?” Weekly review proposes a visible diff; it does not silently rewrite routines.
- **Collaborate:** peers, teachers, coaches, growers and qualified clinicians participate where appropriate, with separately scoped sharing. AI is not the compulsory gatekeeper to another human.
- **Leave easily:** pause, delete, export, revoke, undo; offline and no-wearable paths remain useful. No shame for missed days, coercive streaks or escalating notifications.

Dash/Ripple are the leading integrated review surfaces; OpenDots, OMP, o8, Omarchy and mobile launchers are optional hosts. They must not each grow a parallel scheduler, memory system and dashboard. Select a primary host for each pilot after comparing the real user journey.

## 5. Three vertical slices, with a single first priority

### V1 — Remember and use something learned from real work (first priority)

**Job:** “Turn one lesson from my selected repository or reviewed conversation excerpt into knowledge I can independently use later.”

Input is an explicitly selected public source revision or a private, locally retained excerpt the user approved. Produce up to five proposed cards with claim/source/commit, question, answer, uncertainty and deduplication identity. The user edits/accepts/skips. Export through the existing Anki pathway; do not bypass Anki/FSRS or silently import a whole chat archive.

The useful result is not five generated files. Demonstrate one accepted card in the user's chosen collection through a source-owned receipt, then a separately authored immediate/24-hour/seven-day probe when actually due. Keep card self-rating distinct from objective answer scoring, and retention distinct from transfer to a new task. Reports distinguish NOT_DUE, SKIPPED, MISSING, OBSERVED and independently assessed outcomes.

**Acceptance:** source revision changes invalidate stale claims; duplicate export does not duplicate notes; no approval means no write; revoked data is excluded from future processing; offline failure is visible; held-out probes are unavailable to tutor/candidate search; source links and licenses survive export. First run may use entirely synthetic/public examples and an export file; it must say **no live collection or human learning was tested**.

**Owners:** existing learning workbench and Civilization #231, native Anki/FSRS, Hermes #639, AODL #47, z0evals #104. Do not block this on a new NOOP schema, EEG, GPU training, complete digital twin or game engine.

### V2 — Small action, truthful observation, low-burden review

**Job:** “Help me do my chosen wind-down or focus action without nagging, and show what we can actually learn.”

Reuse NOOP Breathe/Journal, existing two-minute habit work and the chosen UI host. User defines cue/action, grants a bounded scope and interruption budget. Ignoring a reminder creates no behavior observation. Completion requires explicit source-owned acknowledgment. The next-day report shows coverage, source and alternative explanations, not a causal treatment claim. Weekly changes need a visible confirmation.

**Acceptance:** no wearable, stale source, wrong wake-day, timezone/DST boundary, cancel, duplicate handoff, failed write and revoked consent all remain truthful. Static user-chosen cue and no-prompt behavior remain controls. The prospective #42–44 stack stays held; use current primitives until the documented need is demonstrated.

**Owners:** NOOP #70/#72/#76, Hermes #639, AODL #47, Ripple #6, ActivityWatch/Loop where specifically enabled; study search EL #41 and protected eval #104. Do not launch another reminder daemon.

### V3 — ATLAS improves an environment without pretending it improved a person

**Job:** “Compare a small environment/growing/training change, understand the evidence, and choose whether to try it.”

Start with one bounded **non-actuating** scenario in an existing Blueprint/ACE research owner: for example, compare two explicitly simulated growing schedules under identical assumptions, or visualize measured-versus-simulated outcomes of one coaching scenario. Pin geometry/model, solver, units, seeds, scenario and measurement protocol. Label observation, estimate, simulation and hypothesis separately throughout the UI.

First establish a useful physical-domain outcome: resource use, measured crop outcome, repeatable task accuracy or coaching efficiency. A better simulated crop yield is neither observed food composition nor proven human health benefit. Add human-outcome joins only through a separately reviewed prospective study. No camera surveillance, robot commands, hazardous device control or clinician substitution is authorized by this roadmap.

**Acceptance:** compare a simple baseline; reject wrong units, stale revisions, uncalibrated inputs and unsupported extrapolation; show uncertainty and no-data states; reviewer chooses next action. A simulator win must survive relevant measurement before a real-world claim. A 3D view must justify itself against a simpler view.

| Gate | Required proof | Work that can proceed independently |

| G0: stable definitions | Owner map, explicit goals/constraints, permitted inputs, metric units, evidence tier and privacy classification | Source audit, UX prototypes, existing fixes |
| G1: executable contracts | Synthetic negative fixtures reject unauthorized action, missing→No, duplicate identities, late predictions and synthetic→human claims | V1 exporter/fixture, V2 native Journal correctness, V3 units/model fixtures |

| G3: prospective observation | Frozen primary outcome/baseline, genuine delayed measurement, burden/withdrawal reports, appropriate independent review | Public research/counterevidence; no efficacy advertising |

The immediate engineering order is **V1 + G1**, then V2 composition and V3 physical-domain proof. They share contracts but are not forced into one giant release. Current issue/PR maturity is source-reported unless this wave publishes an exact test receipt. A generated roadmap does not advance a gate.

## 7. Minimum interoperability and autoresearch contract

Reuse native schemas through small adapters. Candidate fields below are requirements to map, not permission to introduce a second universal event store:

- **Intent reference:** user goal, boundary revision, permitted effect, expiry, budget, approval/withdrawal and undo policy. AODL/host enforce it.
- **Evidence reference:** source-owned record/session, exact producer revision, observed versus ingested time, unit, measurement version, coverage, privacy class, explicit missingness and correction history.
- **Study reference:** predeclared primary endpoint, comparable baseline/arm, unit of assignment, assignment probability when adaptive, exposure window, lag, planned analysis, stopping rule and frozen evaluator revision.
- **Outcome reference:** self-report/objective/proxy/simulated classification, observed time, assessment method, result/uncertainty, independent verifier and limitations. Receipt shape is not proof the event happened.
- **Candidate reference:** exact algorithm/model/config, seed, source lineage, allowed mutation set, search budget, failures and training/evaluation split.
- **Decision reference:** suggest/ask/abstain, evidence used, constraints checked, human disposition, actual execution and rollback. Decision, authorization, delivery, completion and benefit are distinct events.

Training/evaluation partitioning groups related sessions, items, retries and source families to avoid leakage. A candidate never sees sealed answers. An evaluator version change invalidates unsupported comparisons. “Continuously optimizing” means repeated **bounded, reviewable** cycles, not unbounded unsupervised experimentation on people.

The system must be able to conclude: no useful recommendation; insufficient evidence; candidate worsened burden; ordinary tooling is better; a human expert is the appropriate collaborator; remove the feature.

## 8. Wider repository portfolio without a forced dependency on everything

Every repository we contribute to should have an explicit relationship: **core owner, pilot adapter, later enabling infrastructure, reference-only, or unrelated to this release**. Contributing to a repository does not make it a supported part of this product.

- Human interfaces: desktop/mobile launchers, Noctalia, CUA, o8, OMP, OpenDots and CopilotKit can reduce interaction cost through existing consented host boundaries. They are not prerequisites for the first Anki proof.
- Spatial/physical tooling: OpenUSD/Open3D, Newton/Warp, Gazebo/MuJoCo, Cesium/GEV and facility/building tools are potential observation/simulation/rendering owners when a specific physical-domain case needs them. Never turn a visualization into control authority.
- Environment/resources: agriculture/water/building/energy models, PyPSA and fusion work support a longer ATLAS horizon. They retain separate scientific validation, source licenses and public-interest missions; do not claim a current dependency merely from thematic overlap.
- Compute/runtime: existing placement, infrastructure, GPU and harness projects may make experiments affordable/reproducible. Hardware acceleration is justified by total verified cost/latency, not a new model benchmark in isolation.
- Knowledge/creative tools: public evidence and source-linked media can support understanding; protect held-out tasks and copyright/provenance. Personal transcripts are not automatically training material.

Add an adapter only after naming its consumer, contract, requirement and smallest proof. Keep implementation and promotion in the actual owner repository. This document is not a second factory project registry or authorization for mass issue/PR creation.

## 9. Openness, privacy and claims

Aim for open, reproducible new contracts, fixtures and research artifacts under the owning project's approved license. The present ecosystem is **mixed-license**: NOOP's [license](https://github.com/kvnloo/noop/blob/main/LICENSE) is PolyForm Noncommercial, so the complete current stack must not be advertised as wholly OSI open source. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) prohibits restrictions on fields of endeavor. Keep independently developed adapters/data formats separate; do not copy incompatible implementations or relicense contributors' work. This is a product boundary, not legal clearance.

Public artifacts contain public sources, synthetic examples and separately approved non-sensitive aggregates. Private study configuration, health histories, source snippets, device identifiers and personal timelines stay in the user's chosen private stores. Hashing a sensitive identifier does not automatically anonymize it. No automatic publishing of study data; source withdrawal and deletion have explicit downstream limitations.

Claims use the weakest justified stage: proposed, documented, fixture-tested, integrated, live-used, measured, independently verified, generalized. Negative results count as knowledge. Clinical, pharmacological and high-risk physical interventions are not part of unattended consumer optimization. Qualified governance cannot be replaced by an agent's confidence.
